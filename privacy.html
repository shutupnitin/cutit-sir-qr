Privacy ✦ Keep It Chill

At CutIt.sbs, we believe privacy shouldn’t be complicated. Whether you’re generating a QR code or shortening a link, here’s how we handle your info — short, simple, and transparent:

  • 🔒 No Creepy Tracking
    We don’t sell your data, stalk your clicks, or build secret profiles. Your privacy stays yours.
  • 📦 QR Codes Stay Local
    Everything you generate happens right in your browser. We don’t store or peek at your QR content.
  • 🔗 Short Links Are Minimal
    When you shorten a link, we only save what’s needed to make the redirect work. Nothing more, nothing less.
  • 🕵️ No Snooping on Redirects
    We don’t track who clicks your links or where they go. If you share it, that’s between you and your people.
  • 🍪 Cookies? Only the Basic Kind
    We might use simple cookies for site performance or ads, but never to spy on you.
  • 🚫 Third-Party Reality Check
    Some ads or external links may have their own tracking. That’s outside our control, so check their policies if you care.

In short: we respect your privacy, keep our hands off your data, and only store what’s absolutely necessary to make CutIt.sbs work.
